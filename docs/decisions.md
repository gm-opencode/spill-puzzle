| 10 | 2026-11-20 | Edge curve (spec §4.1–4.2) concretised as generator version 1: RNG draws per edge in the order `dir, center, size, neck, j1, j2`; all horizontal edges row-major, then all vertical edges row-major. `dir = +1` puts the tab on the left-hand side of the canonical direction (horizontal edges left→right, so up; vertical edges top→bottom, so right). | 22 | 2026-11-20 | Model API: an immutable `BoardState` (`groups: Map<id, Group>`, a `pieceGroup` index, `maxZ`). Merge ranking (spec §6.1 step 3): most pieces wins; on a tie the already-placed group beats the dropped one (the dropped piece snaps to what's on the table), then the lower id. | 24 | 2026-11-20 | Scatter (spec §6.6): slots are square cells of `1.35·max(pw, ph)` in rings around the board. The first ring sits a gap of `piecePad` away from the board, so even tabs never cover it. Top/bottom bands span the full width including corners; side bands span the board height; cells are stretched evenly to tile each band. Rings start at `floor(0.6·max(w, h) / slot)` (at least 1) and grow until there are enough slots. Portrait: picks alternate 2 above/below : 1 side (shuffled per band), landscape 1 : 1. Each piece cell is jittered uniformly inside its slot. The random stream uses `seed XOR 0x5ca77e12`, independent of the shape stream | The spec leaves the slot layout and the weighting method open. Using integer interleaving avoids `Math.pow`/`Math.log`, whose results can differ between engines | Pieces never overlap each other or the board (tested for 12–500 pieces). `scatter` also returns the scatter `area` for camera zoom-to-fit (M4) |
